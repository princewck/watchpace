# 让观看与休息更可预期

观有度帮助家长安排电视观看时间。建议先与孩子商量什么时候结束、接下来做什么，再设置每日上限和固定休息时段。规则尽量稳定，避免把暂停、答题、恢复播放变成越来越短的循环。

频繁打断可能带来烦躁、挫败或亲子冲突。如果孩子明显不安、抗拒或难以平静，请暂停这类提醒，与孩子重新商量安排，必要时寻求合适的专业帮助。答题功能不是注意力训练或评估工具。

## 如何设置

- 优先设置每日观看上限和家庭固定休息时段，提前说明结束后的线下活动。
- 如果规则页出现“首次挑战后”“后续每次”等观看阶段，可在“少打扰的休息安排”中预览稳定间隔模板。首次观看保留原有时长，低于20分钟才调至20分钟；首次、第二次及此后的每次答题放行均改为20分钟。例如原来30／20／5／2分钟，应用后为30／20／20／20分钟。确认保存后对家庭内全部电视生效，各电视仍独立计时；保留每日上限、休息时段、连续休息重置时间及默认临时额度，不关闭答题功能。
- 编辑阶段规则时，观看间隔短于15分钟会显示提示，家长仍可确认保存。这是产品提示阈值，不代表任何经过医学验证的安全标准。
- 20分钟模板也只是设置示例，不适合直接套用于所有儿童。请结合年龄、日常作息、内容和实际反应调整。

如果规则页只有每日上限及休息时段，当前服务不使用循环答题，也不显示阶段设置和稳定间隔模板。保存的规则需要电视获取后才能执行；离线行为及权限要求请参阅[安装说明](../installer/family-tv-setup/SKILL.md)。软件无法替代成人照看，也不保证所有电视都能阻止退出或绕过。

# Predictable viewing and breaks

Family TV helps parents plan television time. Agree with your child on when viewing will end and what comes next. Prefer consistent daily limits and scheduled breaks over increasingly short cycles of stopping, answering questions, and resuming.

Frequent interruptions may be frustrating or distressing and may contribute to family conflict. If your child becomes upset, resists the reminders, or struggles to settle, pause these reminders and reconsider the plan together. Seek appropriate professional support when needed. Quizzes are not attention training or assessment tools.

If the rules page shows viewing stages, such as time after a quiz, open “Plan breaks with fewer interruptions” to preview an optional consistent-interval template. It preserves a longer initial viewing period or raises a shorter one to 20 minutes. It changes the time granted after the first, second, and every subsequent quiz to 20 minutes. For example, 30/20/5/2 minutes becomes 30/20/20/20 minutes. Only confirmation saves the changes for all TVs in the family, which still keep separate counters. The template preserves daily limits, scheduled breaks, the continuous-rest reset period, and default extra time; it does not disable quizzes.

Intervals below 15 minutes trigger a warning but can still be saved. Neither the warning threshold nor the 20-minute template is a medically validated safe duration or a recommendation for every child. Adjust the plan for your child's age, daily routine, content, and response. Explain the offline activity that will follow before viewing begins.

If the rules page only shows daily limits and scheduled breaks, the connected service does not use repeated quizzes and does not show stage settings or this template. TVs must receive updated rules before applying them; see the [installation guide](../installer/family-tv-setup/SKILL.md) for offline behavior and permission requirements. The software does not replace adult supervision or guarantee that every TV prevents exiting or bypassing restrictions.
